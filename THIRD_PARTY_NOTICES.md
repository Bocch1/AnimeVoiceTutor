Original characters, artwork, sound recordings, trademarks and other third-party intellectual property belong to their respective rights holders. The project does not claim ownership of these materials, and its software license does not authorize their redistribution.

Rights holders who believe material distributed through AnimeVoiceTutor infringes their rights may contact the maintainer through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Bocch1/AnimeVoiceTutor/issues). Substantiated complaints will be reviewed promptly, and affected material will be removed when appropriate. Please avoid posting private identification documents or personal recordings in public issues; use private contact arrangements for sensitive details.
